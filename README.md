## 🛡️ Flagship Project: აშკარაა (PhishGuard)

**[ 🚀 Launch Live Application ](https://bolt.new/p/72195046)**

### 📌 Vision & Problem Statement
In an era of rising digital deception, social engineering, and targeted phishing attacks, non-tech-savvy users—especially senior citizens—require immediate, zero-friction protection. **აშკარაა** (*"Obvious / Clear"*) was engineered as a high-precision security platform designed to detect, analyze, and neutralize fraud vectors across Georgian & English SMS, email communications, and suspicious web URLs before compromise occurs.

### 🎨 Brand Identity & Accessibility Standards
* **Senior-Centric UI/UX:** Designed with high-contrast color systems, clear typography (minimum 18px body text), and reassuring Georgian language explanations.
* **Design Philosophy:** Architected at the intersection of **Minimalism**, **Industrial Functionalism**, and **Mid-Century Modern** design principles.
* **Visual Systems:** Designed using **Figma** and **Figma Wave** with grid alignment, featuring custom warm 3D illustrations surrounded by a protective security aura.

### ⚡ Core Architecture & Engineering Highlights
* **Rapid Prototyping Engine:** Built and deployed seamlessly utilizing **Bolt.new**, **React**, and **Tailwind CSS**.
* **Visual Risk Meter:** Color-coded threat results (Green for Safe, Yellow for Suspicious, Red for Phishing Scam) with plain-language bullet points explaining *why*.
* **Zero-Knowledge Privacy:** Built with a privacy-first ethos—user queries are evaluated anonymously without storing sensitive messages.
